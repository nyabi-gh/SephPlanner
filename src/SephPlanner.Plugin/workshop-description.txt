[h1]SephPlanner[/h1]
가방에 든 석판과 아티팩트를 어떻게 놓으면 좋은지 계산해서 게임 화면에 보여 주는 모드입니다.

탐험에 들어가면 화면 구석에 지금 가방 점수와 제안 배치 점수가 뜹니다. [b]F7[/b]로 펼치면 자리가 바뀌는 칸에 금색 테두리가 생기고, 칸에 커서를 올리면 왜 그렇게 놓는지 나옵니다. [b]F8[/b]을 누르면 제안대로 옮깁니다. 직접 옮기고 싶으면 이동 안내를 위에서부터 따라 하면 됩니다.

상자·상점·세피라이트에서 무엇을 고를지, 어떤 석판을 합성하고 어디에 인챈트할지도 알려 줍니다. [b]F1[/b]은 아직 얻지 않은 후보를 넣었을 때의 배치를 보여 줍니다. [b]F2[/b]에 게임에서 복사한 프리셋 코드를 붙여 넣거나 노리는 콤보를 고르면 추천이 그 빌드를 따라갑니다.

[h2]단축키[/h2]
F1 후보 미리보기, F2 빌드 설정, F3 설정, F4 숨기기, F5 불투명도, F6 패널 이동, F7 펼치기·접기, F8 배치 적용, F9 아이템 데이터 다시 읽기, F10 오류 제보
키는 F3에서 바꿀 수 있습니다.

[h1]SephPlanner[/h1]
Works out where the Tablets and Artifacts in your inventory should go and shows it on the game screen.

Once you start a run, a corner of the screen shows your current score next to the score of the suggested layout. Press [b]F7[/b] to expand it. Slots that would change get a gold outline, and hovering over a slot tells you why. Press [b]F8[/b] to move everything into place, or follow the move list from the top if you'd rather do it by hand.

It also tells you what to take from chests, shops and Sephirite, which Tablets to combine and where to enchant. [b]F1[/b] shows the layout with a candidate you haven't picked up yet. Paste a preset code copied in the game into [b]F2[/b], or pick the combos you're going for, and the suggestions will follow that build.

[h2]Hotkeys[/h2]
F1 candidate preview, F2 build settings, F3 settings, F4 hide, F5 opacity, F6 move panel, F7 expand/collapse, F8 apply layout, F9 reload item data, F10 bug report
You can rebind them in F3.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The score is SephPlanner's own number for comparing layouts, not an in-game stat.
[*]It only changes your own screen, so other players don't need it. Auto-place is off in multiplayer. Get everyone's OK first, then turn on 'Auto-place in multiplayer' in F3.
[*]The BepInEx (ZIP) version ended with 0.6.6. If you used it, delete SephPlanner.Plugin.dll and SephPlanner.Core.dll from BepInEx/plugins in the game folder, or this version won't load. Your settings carry over the first time it starts.
[*]Made by an individual, not affiliated with TEAM HORAY.
[*]If the game misbehaves, turn this mod off first. If the problem stays with it off, report it to TEAM HORAY; if it goes away, tell me in the comments here or on GitHub Issues.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a problem[/h2]
Press F10 while the problem is happening. It shows what it would send, uploads it to the developer's private server only if you agree, and then shows a report number. Post that number in the comments here or on [url=https://github.com/nyabi-gh/SephPlanner/issues]GitHub Issues[/url].
